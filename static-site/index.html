The Magic Behind the Optimization

Most Kafka consumers are statically configured. But traffic isn't static. KafkaConsumerAutoTune uses a PID (Proportional-Integral-Derivative) controller to solve this.

  • P: Proportional – Reacts to the current error (Difference between actual and target duration).
  • I: Integral – Corrects long-term bias from historical errors.
  • D: Derivative – Anticipates future changes based on current trends.
